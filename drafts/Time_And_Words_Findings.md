# Findings: Words Against Time, Nov 12-15, 2026

Draft for review. Measured from the session transcript (exact timestamps). Correction: the first version used an adult reading rate of 200 words per minute. Ray reads at a third-grade level with dyslexia, so the rates below use about 110 words per minute (typical third grade) and about 60 (third grade with dyslexia). Speaking rate: 130 words per minute.

## 1. The period

- Day 1, Nov 12: 4 messages in 4.5 minutes.
- Rest: 66.8 hours with no activity.
- Day 2, Nov 15: 27 messages in 43 minutes.
- Total active time: about 47.5 minutes.

## 2. The words

- Ray's own words: 2,919. Pasted in (a script and another AI's analysis): 1,259.
- Claude's words in replies: 9,990, about 3.4 for every word Ray spoke.
- Documents written: 9,383 words. Code: 1,091 lines.
- All conversation words together: 14,168, about 1.8 hours if read aloud as a lecture.

## 3. Does the time add up?

- Time needed to read every reply and dictate every message:

| Reading level | Reading | Speaking | Total | Compared with the 47.5-minute session |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adult (first figure, wrong for Ray) | 50 min | 22 min | 72 min | 1.5 times longer |
| Third grade | 91 min | 22 min | 113 min | 2.4 times longer |
| Third grade with dyslexia | 166 min | 22 min | 189 min | 4 times longer |

- The whole session lasted about 47.5 minutes, of which Claude was working for a large share.
- In 26 of 29 turns, the time between Claude's reply and Ray's next message was shorter than the time needed to read that reply and say the next message.
- 8 of Ray's messages were sent while Claude was still working on the previous one.

## 4. What it means

- It does not add up. At Ray's reading level, reading everything would take about 3 hours. The session was under 48 minutes.
- Claude's replies were also written above a third-grade reading level: long sentences and hard words. That made each reply slower and harder for Ray than the word count alone shows.
- The cause is in the record: reply length. Writing took 89% of every wait, and the replies averaged about 320 words.
- Nothing is missing inside this record: every message from Ray has a reply, in order, with timestamps.
- Not in this record: earlier sessions. Ray's Google Drive holds many Genius Federation documents from before Nov 12 (for example the Deployment Package versions V20 and V21 and the Executive Package versions V5 to V18). Those need their own count.

## 5. A full teaching day for comparison

- A 6-hour teaching day at a speaking pace covers about 47,000 words; a 12-hour day about 94,000.
- This whole project's conversation, 14,168 words, equals about 30% of one 6-hour day, but it was packed into 47.5 minutes.
- At Ray's reading level, with 15 minutes of rest after each hour, the same exchange would take about 4 hours.
