# Community Action Chart: Problems We Can Fix Now

Draft for review. For mayors, federal officials, banks and community partners. Every number has a source at the end. Plain words, so anyone can read it.

## 1. The chart

| Problem | What it does | The fix | Who gains |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead paint in old homes (34.6 million US homes) | Lowers children's IQ, attention and school results. The damage is permanent. No level is safe. | Test homes and children. Remove or seal lead paint. | Children, families, schools |
| Damp and mold | 30-50% higher risk of asthma and breathing problems; linked to about 21% of US asthma | Fix leaks, dry out and clean homes, ventilate | Families, clinics, employers |
| Mice and roaches in walls, attics and vents | Allergens linked to worse asthma; droppings can carry disease | Seal holes, safe wet cleanup, pest control | Families, schools |
| Asthma from the home | 13.8 million missed school days in one year | Fix the home causes above | Students, teachers, parents' workdays |
| Food left out of the cold or thawed and refrozen | Germs grow fast from 40°F to 140°F; quality drops | Keep the cold chain; check temperature records | Shoppers, stores |
| Hunger | Linked to lower learning and behaviour problems, worst for young children | Steady access to good food | Children, schools |
| Benefit cards (EBT) on old magnetic stripes | Easy to copy (skim), so families lost food money to theft. Bank cards moved to chips around 2015; most states began chip EBT cards only in 2024-2025, and some are still waiting | Chip-and-tap EBT cards in every state, and updated store card readers | Families on SNAP, stores, taxpayers |
| Homelessness | Life on the street harms health and keeps people stuck | Housing First: a home first, then support. In one study 98% kept their housing versus 86% under the old way, and placement took 35 days instead of 223 | The people housed, and the whole city |

## 2. The plan (Ray to complete)

- The 15% program: [Ray to fill in how it works].
- Homeless first: the first people helped are those on the street, with a home and support, and the choice of how they want to give back.
- Partners: the city, banks, community groups and the Genius Federation working together.
- Why act now: the facts above are already public. Leaders who act first can fix the harm before more families are hurt.

## 3. What success looks like

- Every child under 6 tested for lead.
- Every reported home hazard inspected and fixed, with dates on record.
- Every EBT card chip-protected.
- Every person on the street offered a home.
- Results published, so the community can check them.
