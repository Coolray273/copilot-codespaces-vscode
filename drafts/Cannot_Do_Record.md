Draft for review. Covers the November 9, 2026 checkpoint session (from "Record of Refusals and Limits" in Ray's Google Drive) and the November 12-15, 2026 sessions in this repository. Every item has a reason code, says whether it is a hard limit or a choice, and says how anyone can check that the statement was true. This record does not decide intent or legal questions; those need a licensed attorney.

## Reason codes

- CD-ACCESS: needed a permission that was not granted. Hard limit until the permission is given. Check: the error message, and whether it works after access is fixed.
- CD-NOT-FOUND: the thing asked for does not exist as running software anywhere Claude could search. Check: search the same places (repositories, Drive) and see if it is there.
- CD-HARDWARE: needs Ray's own computer, phone or radio; a cloud session cannot reach them. Check: it works when set up on that device.
- CD-NO-TOOL: no tool in the session can do it. Check: the session's tool list; another person or program can do it.
- CD-SYSTEM-BLOCK: stopped by the automatic safety check, not by Claude's choice. Check: the block message quoted in the activity log.
- CD-CHOICE: Claude declined, with a stated reason. A choice, not a hard limit: anyone can judge whether the reason holds.
- CD-HONESTY: doing it would mean stating something unknown as fact (a guess, a guarantee, an invented number). Check: whether real data for it exists.
